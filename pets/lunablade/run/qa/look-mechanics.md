# Lunablade look mechanics

- Primary direction cue: head and face orientation, especially nose/chin axis and the visible red eye relative to head center.
- Secondary cue: shoulders and saber-hilt angle may follow the head subtly, while the feet and whole body remain registered and upright.
- 000 is unmistakably up/away: back of the white bob is dominant, face is mostly hidden.
- 090 is screen-right: nose/chin and visible eye point to the viewer's right.
- 180 is down/front: face is fully visible and gaze points toward the bottom of the image.
- 270 is screen-left: nose/chin and visible eye point to the viewer's left.
- Intermediate directions interpolate clockwise in even 22.5-degree steps. Do not rotate or tilt the whole sprite to fake gaze.
- Preserve the asymmetric white bob, black-and-white suit, red eye accents, single blue plasma saber, scale, baseline, and compact sticker silhouette.
