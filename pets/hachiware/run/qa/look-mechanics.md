# Hachiware look-direction mechanics

The user-approved official-reference identity is mandatory in all directions:
large round black eyes retain the central white oval and lower white crescent
highlights whenever visible; pink cheeks keep four hatch marks; an open mouth
keeps two small pointed fangs and the pink tongue. The blue forehead marking,
round pear body, tiny limbs, and attached blue tail remain one coherent pet.

- `000` up: the full facial group rises close to the blue marking and the chin
  lifts. Both eyes remain large and highlighted. This must not read as neutral.
- `090` screen-right: a restrained right-facing turn places the visible eye,
  cheek, and mouth on the screen-right edge while keeping the full body inside
  the slot.
- `180` down: the face group moves low, with more blue forehead visible above;
  both eyes aim toward the bottom edge.
- `270` screen-left: a matching restrained left-facing turn places the visible
  eye, cheek, and mouth on the screen-left edge.

Intermediate directions advance clockwise in even 22.5-degree steps. Preserve
the same overall area, baseline, outline weight, eye construction, mouth, and
palette while interpolating face height, horizontal placement, cheek
visibility, ear overlap, and tail position. Do not rotate the whole sprite,
shrink the eyes into beans, delete either eye highlight, delete either fang, or
introduce text, arrows, shadows, effects, or props. The `157.5 -> 180` and
`337.5 -> 000` seams must close without conspicuous scale or identity snaps.
